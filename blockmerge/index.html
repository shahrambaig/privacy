BlockMerge Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

BlockMerge (“the app”) is a puzzle game. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. We do not ask you to create an account.

Information stored on your device

Your high score, progress and settings (such as sound and vibration) are saved only on your device. This data never leaves your device, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

The app currently contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking. If this changes in the future, we will update this policy and the app’s Data safety information before releasing the change.

Permissions

The app uses the vibration permission only to give haptic feedback during gameplay. It uses internet access only as required by the app framework; no personal data is sent.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page.
