Quackline Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Quackline mobile game (“the App”, “we”, “our”) handles information. Quackline is an endless runner game for Android, published on Google Play.

In short: Quackline does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App has no user accounts or sign-in, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data.

The App does not include analytics, tracking, or advertising tools.

Information stored on your device

To save your game, the App keeps a small amount of data only on your own device:

  • your high score and duckling statistics,
  • the duck character you selected,
  • your sound and vibration settings.

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset Progress, or by uninstalling the App.

Permissions

  • Internet: used only to check Google Play for a newer version of the App, so you can be asked to update.
  • Vibrate: used for haptic feedback during gameplay. You can turn this off in Settings.

Third-party services

The App is distributed through Google Play, and uses the Google Play In-App Updates service to check whether an update is available. This check is handled by Google Play on your device and is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not receive any information from this check.

Advertising and purchases

The App currently shows no ads and offers no in-app purchases. If we add them in a future version, we will update this policy before that version is released, describing what data those services use.

Children’s privacy

Quackline is a casual game suitable for all ages. Because the App collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share any user data, because we do not collect any.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
